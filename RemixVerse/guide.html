How the game works

1. Create a lifeChoose a character name and a starting talent. Each new life gets a different city, family and starting stats.
2. Make a choice each yearRead the event and select an available option. Choices can improve or reduce stats, change relationships, affect money, and unlock new opportunities.
3. Manage your energyActivities, career actions and spending time with people may use energy. Your energy restores when you age up.
4. Grow your lifeUse Activities to develop your character, Relationships to strengthen bonds, and Career to apply for jobs, seek promotions or leave a job.
5. Age up to continueAfter making your yearly event choice, press “Celebrate birthday & age up”. A new year begins, money flow is applied and a new event may appear.
6. Build your legacyWhen a life ends, its score, rank and summary are recorded. The Legacy Hall shows past lives and achievements.

Your core stats

Health Affects your wellbeing and survival.

Happiness Reflects how satisfied your character feels.

Intelligence Can support education and career outcomes.

Reputation Reflects how others see your character.

Money Pays for costs and gifts, and can grow through employment.

Saving and privacy

When signed in, LifeLoop saves your current game, completed-life summaries, achievements and preferences to your private cloud account. A local browser cache helps the game stay responsive, while the server keeps the account copy.

Sign in on another device to restore your progress. Use Reset in Legacy only if you intend to erase your saved lives and achievements.
