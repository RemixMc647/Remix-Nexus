About 𝕽𝖊𝖒𝖎𝖝 𝕹𝖊𝖝𝖚𝖘

Last updated: July 2026

𝕽𝖊𝖒𝖎𝖝 𝕹𝖊𝖝𝖚𝖘 (RemixMC) is a free, community-run gaming society for players who would rather squad up than grind alone — Minecraft, Roblox, Fortnite, Call of Duty, Free Fire, PUBG, GTA, Among Us and more.

💬 Live chat rooms📞 Voice & video calls👥 Contacts & DMs🔥 Live & Trending🔎 Search👤 Profiles
Chat features

Create your own room, reply to a specific message (swipe left or right-click), see typing indicators and online status, and start 1:1 or squad calls.

Good to know

Messages disappear after 3 days. Anyone can create a room, but only the site owner can delete one (default rooms are permanent) or remove any message. You can always delete your own. Video uploads are capped at 30 minutes. Allow mic, camera and notifications for calls and alerts.

Notifications

You get desktop notifications while RemixMC is open. The Android app also sends push notifications for messages and calls when closed or locked.

Our approach

Lightweight and free: no pay-to-win and no locked rooms. We run ads to cover hosting costs — see the Terms and Privacy Policy.

Feedback, bugs or room ideas? Use our Contact page — we read every message.
